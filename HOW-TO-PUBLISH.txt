PUT VOLUME BRUSH ON YOUR OWN FREE WEBSITE (GitHub Pages) - then use it on the iPad
=================================================================================

One time setup (about 5 minutes, no coding):

1. Go to https://github.com and create a free account (remember your user name).

2. Click "+" (top right) -> "New repository".
   - Repository name:  volume-brush
   - Choose "Public"   (free websites need a public repository: people who know the
     link could open the tool, but your drawings always stay on your own device)
   - Click "Create repository".

3. On the new page click the link "uploading an existing file"
   (or later: "Add file" -> "Upload files").
   Open this "website" folder on your PC, select EVERYTHING INSIDE it
   (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, the .png icons, .nojekyll) and drag it
   into the browser window. Then click "Commit changes".
   Tip: ".nojekyll" is a hidden file - in Explorer use View -> Show -> Hidden items.
   (If it is missing, the site still works.)

4. In the repository click "Settings" -> "Pages" (left side).
   Source: "Deploy from a branch".  Branch: "main"  and folder "/ (root)".  Click "Save".

5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your tool is now online at:
       https://YOUR-USER-NAME.github.io/volume-brush/

On the iPad:
6. Open that address in Safari.
7. Tap Share (square with arrow) -> "Add to Home Screen" -> Add.
   Now Volume Brush opens from the Home Screen in full screen, like an app.
   The iPad needs internet (3D engine and fonts load from the internet).

Updating later:
- After a change on the PC, run build.ps1, then on github.com open the repository,
  "Add file" -> "Upload files", drop the new index.html and commit.
  The website updates within a minute or two (reload the page on the iPad).

Using it: Apple Pencil paints with pressure, 1 finger moves the sheet,
2 fingers zoom, 2-finger tap = undo, 3-finger tap = redo.
Exports open the share sheet -> "Save to Files" or "Save Image".
